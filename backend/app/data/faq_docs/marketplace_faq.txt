FARMER MARKETPLACE - CUSTOMER HELP

What is Farmer Marketplace?
Farmer Marketplace is a platform that connects farmers directly with customers to buy agricultural products.

How do I register?
You can create an account by selecting Register and entering your name, email, password, role, and phone number.

What account roles are available?
The platform supports two roles: farmer and customer.

How do I log in?
Go to the Login page and enter your registered email and password.

How do I browse products?
Go to the Products page to view available marketplace products.

How do I search for products?
You can use the product search feature to find products based on their name or related terms.

How do I add products to my cart?
Open a product and select the option to add the product to your cart.

How do I place an order?
Add the required products to your cart, proceed to checkout, and create your order.

What happens when I place an order?
The system creates an order with pending status and calculates the total amount.

Can I see my order status?
Currently, newly created orders have a pending status.

Who sells products on the marketplace?
Products are added and sold by farmers registered on the platform.

What is the AI product recommendation feature?
The AI recommendation feature finds products that are semantically related to a customer's search query.

What is the Farming Assistant?
The Farming Assistant is an AI feature designed to answer farming and agricultural questions using the platform's farming knowledge base.

What should I do if I cannot find a product?
Try searching with different keywords or browse the available products.

Is this marketplace focused on South Indian agriculture?
The farming knowledge features prioritize South Indian agricultural information, while customers can browse products available on the marketplace.

Is registration free?
Yes. Users can register on the Farmer Marketplace platform by creating an account with their required details.

Can farmers and customers use the same account?
No. During registration, users select their role as either farmer or customer.

What information is required for registration?
Users need to provide their name, email address, password, role, and optionally a phone number.

What should I do if I cannot log in?
Make sure you are using the correct registered email address and password. If the problem continues, register only if you do not already have an account.

How are product prices displayed?
Each product in the marketplace displays its price along with its product information.

What happens if a product is out of stock?
An order cannot be successfully created for a quantity greater than the available product stock.

Can I choose the quantity of a product?
Yes. The quantity requested is included when creating an order, subject to available stock.

What happens if I order more than the available stock?
The system returns an insufficient stock message and the order is not created for that unavailable quantity.

How does direct farmer-to-customer buying help?
The marketplace is designed to connect farmers directly with customers and provide a direct platform for agricultural products.

How does AI product search work?
AI product search uses semantic search to find products related to the meaning of a customer's query, rather than relying only on exact keyword matches.

What are AI product recommendations?
The recommendation system finds marketplace products that are semantically related to the customer's query.

Can I ask the platform questions about farming?
Yes. The Farming Assistant is available for farming and agricultural questions using the platform's agricultural knowledge base.

What is the difference between the Farming Assistant and Customer Assistant?
The Farming Assistant answers agricultural and farming questions. The Customer Assistant helps users understand marketplace features such as registration, products, orders, and AI features.

Can I browse products without using AI search?
Yes. Users can browse the available products directly from the Products page.

What happens after an order is created?
The system calculates the total order amount, reduces the available product stock, and creates the order with a pending status.
